Final Project: Design of a 55–62 GHz Three-Stage Cascode Low-Noise Amplifier Using the IHP Open-Source 130-nm PDK Technology

Designed and simulated a wideband three-stage cascode low-noise amplifier (LNA) using the IHP open-source 130-nm PDK, QUCS-S, XSCHEM, KLayout, and ADS. Achieved 30.4 dB peak gain at 58 GHz, 5.48–5.9 dB noise figure, < −10 dB return loss, and 58.2 mW power consumption, demonstrating performance comparable to commercial PDKs.

Design of a Low Noise Amplifier (LNA) Circuit With a Common-Source Resistive Feedback Topology for the 3.6–3.8 GHz Frequency Range

Designed, mathematically analyzed, and simulated a Low Noise Amplifier (LNA) operating in the 3.6–3.8 GHz frequency band using a common source resistive feedback topology.
